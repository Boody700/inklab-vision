## Dataset

Alpaydin, E. & Kaynak, C. (1998). *Optical Recognition of Handwritten Digits* [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C50P49.

Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/80/optical+recognition+of+handwritten+digits. UCI publishes this dataset under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

Included original materials: official `optdigits.tra`, `optdigits.tes` and `optdigits.names`. Adaptations include normalized/exported classifier parameters, the browser pixel editor, evaluation reports and transformed image examples.
